Game idea : clicker game where instead of clicking, players roast marshmallows over a campfire in the wild as a currency and score. Players can use these marshmallows to buy upgrades to increase marshmallow output and production

Game mechanics 
Roasting marshmallows: players hold spacebar to roast a marshmallow. It takes 1.5 seconds to roast a marshmallow normally, and there is a time frams of 1-2.3 seconds for it to be considered roasted, if players release too early it will not count.  but if players are patient enough, if they continue holding it until it reaches 2.5 seconds, marshmallow will turn golden-brown, showing that it is a good marshmallow, and instead of counting as one marshmallow it will count as 2, or twice the marshmallows per roast. However there is a small time frame to get golden brown only from 2.3 to 2.5 seconds if player releases too early it will count as one and if too late the marshmallow will turn black, shrivel to dust and count as 0 marshmallows.
Some rules for shop upgrades: price increase formula: every time a player buys a upgrade, the price of the ugrade is multiplied by 1.25 times the price at which the player bought it for, so prices will grow infinitely higher. Tier rule: a player can only unlock the next tier of upgrade after buying ten of an upgrade, like you can only buy +10 mpr upgrade after you have bought 10 +1 mpr upgrades. 

Increasing marshmallows per roast (mpr): players can increase mpr by buying upgrades from the shop. Every time they purchase an upgrade, players mpr will increase and every time they roast a marshmallow they get mre marshmallows they can roast, for example if they buy an upgrade of. +1 mpr while their current mpr is 1, evry time the roast a marshmallow they get 2 marshmallows. This can stack so if you buy say 10 mpr upgrades of +1 mpr, every time you roast u get 11 marshmallows. If you roast these marshmallows till golden, the amount of marshmallows you get is your current mpr  times 2 so if you have 5 mpr and get a golden marshmallow you get 10 marshmallows per roast. The price of these upgrades will increase with every purchase, with +1 pr starting at 20 marshmallows, and every time price increases by 1.25 so the next upgrade will cost 25 marshmallows and so on. There are also more mpr upgrades, with +10 mpr with start price of 150 marshmallows, +50 mpr starting price of 400 marshmallows, +100 mpr at 800 marshmallows and +500 mpr at 4000 marshmallows. The same formula of price increasing by 1.25 still applies to these tiers of mpr upgrades. 

Marshmallows on sticks: obviously, a player cannot have 500 marshmallows on a single stick, so there are stick upgrades too. A singular stick can fit 5 marshmallows at maximum, meaning that you maximum mpr is your current number of sticks times 5, but you can buy more sticks in shop. One stick costs 50 marshmallows, and lets you have 5 more mpr. 5 sticks cost 200, 10 costs 450, 50 costs 2000, 100 costs 4500. The same price increase formula of 1.25 times every time you buy an upgrade still applies so price will not remain at starting price stated above. The tier rule also applies of buying 10 before unlocking next tier
Marshmallows per second (mps): players can also subconsciously earn marshmallows without having to roast them by buying upgrades. When you increase your mps, a friend appears next to you and helps you roast marshmallows. This friend can at most roast 50 marshmallows per second, also increaesd through upgrades. You can increase mps in a few ways: 
increase number of friends. This increases the number of friends helping you roast marshmallows per second, with each friend roasting a maximum of 50 marshmallows per second. U can upgrade ths by buying upgrades in shop, with +1 friend costs 50 marshmallows, +5 costs 200, +10 costs 450, +50 costs 2000, +100 costs 4500, with price increase formula of 1.25 increase per buy applied. Tier rule applies
increase marshmallows each friend roasts (max mpr of friend aka f-mpr) if you increase fmpr by 1, you get fmpr number of marshmallows per second. For example if your fmpr is 2, you get 2 marshmallows a second. This applies to every friend you have meaning if u bought a friend and then increased fmpr by 1, then bought another friend both would have the same fmpr so instead of 3 mps you have 4. Prices for increasing fmpr are the same prices as increasing player mpr, just 1.5 times the starting amount, with the price increase formula of 1.25 times price per buy applied.tier rule applies
To make it less confusing, friends can have 50 marshmallows on a stick and players do not need to buy more sticks for friends
Increase quality of friends: at the basic level, friends can only roast a normal marshmallow, and cannot roast golden marshmallows. However, there is a upgrade where players can let friends get golden marshmallows and double their mps. This upgrade costs 1m marshmallow and is only unlocked after a player has bought 25 of the highest tier of number of friends upgrades, meaning 25 of the  +100 friends ugrades, unlocked after buying 10 of the lower tiered upgrades. This upgrade can only be bought once and applies to all friends, past present and future.

Game graphics: player sits in first person view in front of a campfire at night in the woods next to a tent at the side, surrounded by dark trees. Vibe is meant to be chill and comfortable. When roasting marshmallows, players stick in hand turns clockwise with hand turning it, ensuring marshmallows are roasted on all sides. When roasting marshmallows, there is a bar at the side of the marshmallow stick and you can see the doneness of the marshmallow as the bar increases from down to up. Make a section of the bar golden to show the point of golden marshmallow and a black part at the very top to show burnt marshmallow. When marshmallow is being roasted show the goldenness as it slowly gets cooked, and it bursts into flames when it gets burnt. If player continues to roast marshmallow after it is burnt, after 2 seconds, player gets burnt and dies.
There is also a sied menu to show the shop, with two tabs, one for player mpr and one for friend mpr (mps), each tab showing the upgrades and cost. This can be collapsible with an arrow at the side, ith animations of the tab sliding in and out when closed and opened
At the top left corner of screen left of menu bar, show amount of marshmallows (score) and time played in in-game nights (one night equals 10 minutes) 
When player buys another stick, show player holding another stick in hand next to previous sticks, and when they buy more marshmallows per stick also shows the marshmallows on sticks (maximum 5 per stick), marshmallow cooking animations apply. 
When players buy more friends, show a friend appearing and sitting on a foldable chair next to the player. When entire circle is filled, friends start another circle behind player, going clockwise. Marshmallow cooking animations still apply to the friends roasting marshmallows.
